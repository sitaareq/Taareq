05 / My Process

How every project unfolds.

01
Discover

Deep dive into your goals, users, and competitors. Typically 1–2 days. No assumptions — only clarity.

02
Define

User flows, wireframes, and IA mapped before any visuals. Catches 80% of problems early — saves weeks later.

03
Design

High-fidelity UI with interactive Figma prototypes. Every state designed — empty, error, loading, success.

04
Deliver

Developer-ready files, annotated specs, exported assets. Most projects ship in 2–4 weeks. Post-launch support included.
